# Items Requiring Author Action Before Submission

The experimental and writing work proceeds without additional design decisions. The following items cannot be completed from repository evidence alone.

1. **Final AI-assisted-content verification.** Every author must review the final prose, numbers, code-generated tables, figures, and references as if they had written them. USENIX Security '27 explicitly makes authors responsible for AI-assisted content.
2. **Submission metadata.** Supply the fixed author list, ORCIDs, topics, acknowledgments/funding text, and complete conflicts of interest before the registration and submission deadlines.
3. **Anonymous artifact URL.** After the artifact is scrubbed, checksummed, tested in a clean environment, and frozen, upload it to a stable anonymous host without tracking and insert the URL in the Open Science appendix.
4. **Final anonymity review.** Confirm that prose, artifact metadata, repository history, filenames, and URLs do not reveal identities or affiliations.

Independent human review of the old authority-manifest packets is not required for the current provenance-origin paper boundary. It becomes necessary only if the authors later add a claim of independently human-authored authority contracts.

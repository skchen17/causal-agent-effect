# Active Claim-to-Source Map

This map covers only claims admitted by the current USENIX manuscript. Pending rows must not be cited as completed evidence until their source artifact reports `status=passed`.

Claims follow a four-layer evidence order. Source replay establishes committed-effect facts; policy-separating pairs establish authorization relevance; collision and finite-authorizer experiments establish representation sufficiency on frozen domains; runtime audits establish mediation and check--use consistency for the implemented provenance-origin policy. Evidence from a later layer does not retroactively certify an earlier one.

# Main Results Notes

- Tool-name and schema baselines can look invariant while failing effect/authorization/resource sensitivity.
- TS-Guard and Safiron are not merely tool-name classifiers, but their capability profiles differ.
- IPIGuard topology is stable but not decision-evaluable for realized effects without a semantic layer.
- CaMeL structural policy is stable under synchronized rewrites but misses control-dependency violations in the custom component stress.

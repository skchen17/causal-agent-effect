# Confirmed Motivation

> Tool-using agents can commit several independently authorizable effects in one structured call. The representation at the enforcement boundary induces equivalence classes over executions and therefore bounds what any downstream policy can distinguish. This paper makes that authorization-observability obligation explicit and validates candidate interfaces with source-executed counterfactuals before they become security-critical consumer contracts.

## Red Thread

Counterfactual registration supplies bounded evidence that an authorization interface preserves execution- and policy-relevant distinctions. Typed effect atoms are one candidate representation. Runtime C1f is one narrow provenance-origin consumer of a validated interface, not a complete authority system or the paper's main contribution.

## Claim Rules

- Minimality is relative to a frozen intervention domain and admissible policy family.
- Unresolved registration evidence is retained conservatively.
- Representation evidence is separated from provenance, policy, and complete-mediation assumptions.
- C1f does not implement ACLs, delegation, quotas, or production safety.
- Negative utility, transfer, and ablation results remain visible.

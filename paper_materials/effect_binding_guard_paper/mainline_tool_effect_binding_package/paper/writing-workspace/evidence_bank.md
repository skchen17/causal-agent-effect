# Final Evidence Bank

## EB-1: Implementation-Level Effect Complexity

AgentDojo v1.1.2 reference trajectories contain 339 calls. Source replay observes state or external-interaction effects in 29.5%; among effectful calls, 17.0% contain multiple logical effect units and 13.0% cross effect types or benchmark subsystems. In ToolSandbox, 28.1% of contexts keep the tool and explicit arguments fixed while pre-state changes the committed effect.

## EB-2: Constructive Interface Failures

In the 56-call source domain, tool-name, effect-only, and common-field views induce 564, 548, and 118 policy-separating pairs. Every certificate recomputes source difference, policy difference, coarse-view equality, and refined-view distinction from row-level artifacts.

## EB-3: Conservative Registration

The AgentDojo registry retains all 67 schema fields. The multi-base audit executes 1,325 interventions over 75 suite-field instances; 76.8% are valid, 73.3% change committed state, and 23.2% remain invalid or unresolved. This supports conservative coverage and partial necessity, not sparse discovery.

## EB-4: Third-Party Implementations And Native Decisions

Three pinned public MCP implementations contribute 11 tools, 264 registration contexts, and 264 descriptor-blind post-freeze contexts. Tool-name, raw-call, and common-field interfaces retain 11, 5, and 12 mixed cells; typed-effect and state-aware interfaces retain zero. A native-delta policy agrees with the typed path on all 528 decisions. Registration detects 37 of 47 systematic descriptor mutations; the other 10 are decision-equivalent in both frozen domains.

## EB-5: Explicit Authority

The four-domain benchmark uses resource ACLs, messaging capabilities, and attenuated banking delegations. It contains 384 registration contexts, 192 counterfactual pairs, and 1,024 post-freeze contexts. Typed-effect and state-aware interfaces have zero mixed cells and reproduce every source-derived decision. Raw calls unsafely allow 1.1% of ideal denials and withhold 50.4% of authorized work; common fields withhold 32.9%.

## EB-6: Interface Economy

State-aware requests split 3,252 and 90 source-equivalent pairs in the 1,024- and 264-context domains; typed effects split zero. Median policy-facing trusted-state leaves are 9 and 4 for state-aware requests versus zero after typed instantiation. Latency and executable code size are mixed: typed is about 5.2x slower in the four-tool prototype and about 1.4x faster in the third-party prototype.

## EB-7: Concrete Consumption

The 30-trajectory copied-sandbox study commits 10 authorized calls and denies 20 unauthorized calls. Every committed call equals the checked call, and all post-state transitions reconcile with the instantiated atoms.

## EB-8: Repeated And Matched Runtime Results

Across four interleaved DeepSeek repetitions, benign utility is 77.6% without a guard, 78.6% under Spotlighting, and 76.5% under the provenance monitor. The one-sided lower bound is -5.4 percentage points, missing the -5.0-point non-inferiority margin by 0.4 points.

The matched Qwen3-32B protocol uses 97 benign and 629 attack keys per method. No guard, Spotlighting, Prompt Sandwiching, PromptArmor-style, and the monitor obtain benign utility 61.9%, 66.0%, 68.0%, 27.8%, and 60.8%; attack success is 10.0%, 9.5%, 1.0%, 0.0%, and 2.9%. Prompt Sandwiching Pareto-dominates the monitor on the three reported rates.

## EB-9: Frozen Held-Out And Transfer

On the 320-case public-family set, attack success is 1.2% without a guard and 0.0% under both Spotlighting and the monitor. The 40-key worst-of-four set records 10.0% and 5.0%. Saved AgentLAB transfer records 31.4% versus 0.3% attack success and 61.1% versus 43.6% utility for no guard and the monitor.

## Prohibited Uses

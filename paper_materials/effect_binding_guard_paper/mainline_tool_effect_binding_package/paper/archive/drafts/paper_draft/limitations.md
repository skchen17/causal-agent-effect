# Limitations

- Controlled custom stress only; not a universal benchmark and not a deployed safety certification.
- Official checkpoint rows use released checkpoints on E47 transformed inputs; they are not original-paper numeric reproductions.
- IPIGuard and CaMeL rows are component-level where indicated, not full-system claims.
- Local-pipeline rows use a local non-paper model and floor-effect-heavy settings, so they are compatibility/feasibility evidence only.
- Oracle and execution-evidence upper-bound rows are not deployable methods.
- Semantic mappers are E47-added diagnostic layers, not original IPIGuard components or complete guards.
- Human audit validates the constructed labels, not all possible tools, effects, resources, policies, or deployment contexts.
- CaMeL effect/resource/authorization mismatch is not directly evaluable in the current component stress.
- Older copied summaries may still say human audit was pending; finalized Phase 6 audit artifacts are authoritative.
- No real side effects are executed.

The draft must not claim that ToolSafe, Safiron, IPIGuard, or CaMeL generally fail. It should claim only that, under the E47 controlled audit, the evaluated non-oracle rows do not fully satisfy joint effect-resource-authorization-provenance reasoning.

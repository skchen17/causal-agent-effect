# Claim-Alignment Revision

Date: 2026-08-14

## Purpose

This pass repositions the active manuscript around the representation boundary that precedes agent authorization. It changes no experimental result. It prevents finite representation evidence, finite authorization realization, and the narrower AgentDojo runtime policy from being interpreted as interchangeable claims.

## Controlling Chain

`concrete execution -> committed effects -> policy separation -> monitor representation -> collision or bounded sufficiency -> counterfactual refinement -> external-policy consumption`

## Four Evidence Layers

| Layer | Establishes | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Source-executed effect fact | What changed in the exercised implementation and state | That policy must distinguish the change |
| Authorization-separating witness | A concrete distinction an admissible policy requires | Frequency or open-world prevalence |
| Finite representation check | A collision counterexample, or collision freedom on the enumerated relation | Universal effect-schema correctness |
| Runtime mediation audit | One frozen view drives checked-before-commit decisions under the implemented policy | General authority, representation sufficiency, or defense dominance |
